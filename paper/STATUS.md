`main.tex` is the single authoritative manuscript source (Draft 4.1, LaTeX end-to-end).
The PDF is generated by CI (`.github/workflows/paper.yml`) on every paper change and
attached as a build artifact; the released PDF will be committed here at submission.
Build locally with `make` in this directory (requires latexmk). The CI also fails on
unresolved placeholders. `correction_bracket_draft2.md` is historical only.
